# Acquisition

- To obtain your first amulet, you need an <ref item="anvilcraft:amulet_box"/>:
  1. Have an <ref item="anvilcraft:amulet_box"/> in your inventory with a Totem of Undying inside
  2. Take specific fatal damage and have the Totem of Undying consumed to block it
  3. 20% chance to obtain the corresponding amulet; each failure adds an additional 10% until it succeeds
- Afterwards, amulets can be replicated in the <ref item="anvilcraft:jewelcrafting_table"/>; you must obtain the first one before replication

# Activation

- Amulets held in the main hand or offhand are active (holding two activates both)
- The [Amulet Box](100_amulet_box.md) can hold amulets; holding an Amulet Box in your main hand or offhand activates all amulets inside
- Amulets in curio/accessory slots from accessory mods are active

# Amulet List

## <ref item="anvilcraft:emerald_amulet"/>

**Acquisition**: Fatal damage from Iron Golems or Illagers

**Effect**: Villager trades grant a discount; the Iron Golem will not be angered by the player, even if the player attacks it or a villager

<recipe id="anvilcraft:jewel_crafting/emerald_amulet"/>

## <ref item="anvilcraft:topaz_amulet"/>

**Acquisition**: Fatal damage from lightning or a Tesla Tower

**Effect**: Immune to all lightning damage; grants Haste I

<recipe id="anvilcraft:jewel_crafting/topaz_amulet"/>

## <ref item="anvilcraft:ruby_amulet"/>

**Acquisition**: Fatal damage from fire, lava, magma blocks and other hot-floor blocks, or lasers

**Effect**: Grants Fire Resistance; grants Strength I, or Strength II while burning

<recipe id="anvilcraft:jewel_crafting/ruby_amulet"/>

## <ref item="anvilcraft:sapphire_amulet"/>

**Acquisition**: Fatal damage from drowning, Guardians, or Elder Guardians

**Effect**: Grants Conduit Power; grants Resistance I while in water

<recipe id="anvilcraft:jewel_crafting/sapphire_amulet"/>

## <ref item="anvilcraft:anvil_amulet"/>

**Acquisition**: Fatal damage from anvils, a Giant Anvil ground slam, or an Anvil Hammer

**Effect**: Complete immunity to anvil damage; immune to knockback, Levitation, and the gravity of celestial bodies from the Celestial Forging Anvil

<recipe id="anvilcraft:jewel_crafting/anvil_amulet"/>

## <ref item="anvilcraft:comrade_amulet"/>

**Acquisition**: Fatal damage from other players (requires no squad or the same squad; damage from players on different teams does not trigger)

**Effect**: Can be right-click signed by multiple players; when carried and active, grants immunity to damage from signed players

<recipe id="anvilcraft:jewel_crafting/comrade_amulet"/>

## <ref item="anvilcraft:feather_amulet"/>

**Acquisition**: Fatal fall damage

**Effect**: When active, immune to fall damage; grants Slow Falling, which is canceled while holding Shift

<recipe id="anvilcraft:jewel_crafting/feather_amulet"/>

## <ref item="anvilcraft:cat_amulet"/>

**Acquisition**: Fatal damage from Creepers or Phantoms

**Effect**: Repels Creepers and Phantoms; allows wild cats to be tamed bare-handed

<recipe id="anvilcraft:jewel_crafting/cat_amulet"/>

## <ref item="anvilcraft:dog_amulet"/>

**Acquisition**: Fatal damage from Skeletons, Strays, Wither Skeletons, or Bogged

**Effect**: Repels skeletons and prevents them from attacking the wearer; allows wolves to be tamed bare-handed

<recipe id="anvilcraft:jewel_crafting/dog_amulet"/>

## <ref item="anvilcraft:silence_amulet"/>

**Acquisition**: Fatal damage from a Warden

**Effect**: The player does not produce vibrations detectable by Sculk Sensors or Wardens; immune to Darkness

<recipe id="anvilcraft:jewel_crafting/silence_amulet"/>

## <ref item="anvilcraft:abnormal_amulet"/>

**Acquisition**: Fatal damage from the Wither effect (requires simultaneously carrying four items that produce corresponding abnormal effects: 1*Cursed Gold + 64*Floating Powder or Block + Neutron Ingot + 1152*Radioactive items (Plutonium/Uranium))

**Effect**: The player no longer receives abnormal effects from carrying items with abnormal statuses; immune to all negative effects caused by eating

<recipe id="anvilcraft:jewel_crafting/abnormal_amulet"/>

## <ref item="anvilcraft:armadillo_amulet"/>

**Acquisition**: Fatal damage from Spiders

**Effect**: Repels Spiders; grants Resistance II while holding Shift

<recipe id="anvilcraft:jewel_crafting/armadillo_amulet"/>
