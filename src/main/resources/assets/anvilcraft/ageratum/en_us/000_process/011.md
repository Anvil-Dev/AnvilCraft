<row halign="center">
<item id="anvilcraft:heat_collector"/>
<item id="anvilcraft:void_energy_collector"/>

Set a small goal for the power output of your industrial area, such as 3072kW. The <ref item="anvilcraft:heat_collector"/> and <ref item="anvilcraft:void_energy_collector"/> are suitable for achieving this goal

# Experience Logistics

<item id="anvilcraft:exp_collector"/>

- Pair the <ref item="anvilcraft:frost_anvil"/> with a *cobblestone generator* to automate experience orb production
- Add an <ref item="anvilcraft:exp_collector"/> to collect it and store it as liquid
- Use a <ref item="anvilcraft:menger_sponge"/> to boost tank capacity and store large quantities of these liquids
- Solidify it into <ref item="anvilcraft:exp_gem"/> for yourself or villagers
