# 11-Industrial Upgrade

## Power Generation

Set a small goal for the power output of your industrial area, such as 3072kW. The following methods are suitable for achieving this goal:

- [Thermal Power Generation](../003_power/201_heat_collection.md)
- [Void Energy Generation](../003_power/201_void_energy_collection.md)

## Experience Logistics

- Pair the <ref item="anvilcraft:frost_anvil"/> with a *cobblestone generator* to automate experience orb production
- Add an <ref item="anvilcraft:exp_collector"/> to collect it and store it as liquid
- Use a <ref item="anvilcraft:menger_sponge"/> to boost tank capacity and store large quantities of these liquids
- Solidify it into <ref item="anvilcraft:exp_gem"/> for yourself or villagers

## Enchanting Factory

- Use [Liquid Enchantment](../002_material/210_liquid_enchantment.md) to automate the production and duplication of enchantments

## Weapon Platform

- Craft an <ref item="anvilcraft:energy_weapon_platform"/>
