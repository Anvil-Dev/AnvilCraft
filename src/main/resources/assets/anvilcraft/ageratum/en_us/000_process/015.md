> Manual collection is also viable (probably), but what player can resist the allure of automation?

## Mass Producing <ref item="anvilcraft:enchanted_gold_block"/>

1. Use the [Experience Logistics](011.md#experience-logistics) you set up earlier
2. The <ref item="anvilcraft:cinerite"/> produced by the <ref item="anvilcraft:mineral_fountain"/> can be sifted to obtain <ref item="minecraft:lapis_lazuli"/>
3. Refine blank liquid enchantment with <ref item="minecraft:emerald"/> or <ref item="anvilcraft:transcendium_ingot"/> **(Optional)** to consume less
4. Craft <ref item="anvilcraft:enchanted_gold_ingot"/>

## Mass Producing <ref item="anvilcraft:negative_matter_block"/>

1. Build a sand farm next to the End portal, letting sand fall into the End where it transforms into <ref item="anvilcraft:end_dust"/>
2. Collect and sift near the End obsidian platform to obtain <ref item="anvilcraft:levitation_powder"/>
3. If conditions allow, try keeping the End loaded and building a dedicated collider to continuously produce <ref item="anvilcraft:negative_matter_block"/>

## Mass Producing <ref item="anvilcraft:transcendium_block"/>

1. First mass-produce <ref item="anvilcraft:neutronium_ingot"/> and <ref item="anvilcraft:ember_metal_block"/>. Try the manual's built-in [late-game iron farm](../009_machine/320_more_iron.md)
2. If you have automated enchanting, applying more enchantments to <ref item="anvilcraft:charged_neutronium_ingot"/> will significantly increase output and has a chance to return <ref item="anvilcraft:neutronium_ingot"/>
3. Be extremely careful not to let shockwaves from the explosion chamber destroy your automation blocks

## Extracting Planetary Resources

Forged planets contain various resources, allowing you to build planetary collection facilities

With enough luck, you might encounter life-bearing planets, becoming their deity and enjoying their offerings

## Mastering Stellar Energy

- Build a *Ring Collider* to quickly complete collision recipes
- Build a *Dyson Sphere* to obtain massive amounts of energy

## Stellar Evolution

<item id="anvilcraft:stellar_evolution_accelerator_component"/>

- Consumes quite a few <ref item="anvilcraft:corrupted_beacon"/>, try to automate:
  - Since wither skeletons cannot be captured by <ref item="anvilcraft:resin_block"/>, settle for capturing regular skeletons to create spawners
  - Use <ref item="anvilcraft:transcendence_anvil"/> to infinitely stack Beheading enchantment levels, killing skeletons to obtain large amounts of <ref item="minecraft:skeleton_skull"/>
  - Combined with <ref item="minecraft:coal"/>, time-shift them into <ref item="minecraft:wither_skeleton_skull"/>
  - <ref item="anvilcraft:ember_metal_block"/> series building blocks are not destroyed by withers, so create cages to quickly defeat withers and obtain <ref item="minecraft:nether_star"/>
  - Finally produce large quantities of <ref item="minecraft:nether_star"/>, then craft <ref item="anvilcraft:corrupted_beacon"/>
- [Stellar evolution](../004_block/401_celestial_forging_anvil.md#stellar-evolution) is the necessary means to obtain *black holes* and *neutron stars*

<danger>
Ending evolution will produce a massive explosion. Ensure there are no important machines within more than ten blocks
</danger>

## Until the End

- You can now build [wormholes](../004_block/402_teleportation.md) to complete ultra-distance teleportation
- You can also build a *Matter Decompressor* to obtain large amounts of <ref item="anvilcraft:neutronium_ingot"/> and <ref item="anvilcraft:excited_state_void_matter"/>
