# 14-Stepping into Eternity

## <ref item="anvilcraft:charged_neutronium_ingot"/>

Crafting <ref item="anvilcraft:charged_neutronium_ingot"/> is extremely power-hungry. Try using <ref item="anvilcraft:plutonium_block"/> or obtaining large amounts of <ref item="anvilcraft:negative_matter_block"/>

## Transcendium Alloy

1. Smash nuclear fuel blocks to heat up an <ref item="anvilcraft:ember_metal_block"/> and obtain an <ref item="anvilcraft:overheated_ember_metal_block"/>
2. Quickly smash the <ref item="anvilcraft:charged_neutronium_ingot"/> into the <ref item="anvilcraft:overheated_ember_metal_block"/> to obtain <ref item="anvilcraft:transcendium_ingot"/>

## Smithing Template

1. Craft an <ref item="anvilcraft:eight_to_one_smithing_template"/> and apply multiple enchantments to it
2. Destroy the template to trigger [Template Dissociation](../004_block/221_ember_smithing_table.md#template-dissociation) and obtain up to four smithing templates
