# Landing on the Moon

1. Build a vertical [accelerator](../004_block/215_large_electromagnet.md#anvil-impact-crafting)
2. At midnight, launch an <ref item="minecraft:ender_pearl"/> skyward at extremely high speed to teleport to the Moon

# Lunar Giant Monolith

1. Touch it to return to the Overworld spawn
2. Hold a <ref item="anvilcraft:giant_anvil"/> and right-click the monolith core near the lunar spawn, consuming the <ref item="anvilcraft:giant_anvil"/> to obtain information about a random [hidden celestial body](../004_block/401_celestial_forging_anvil.md#hidden-celestial-bodies)

# Lunar Surface

Biomes where the X and Y coordinates are both less than 1024 and greater than -1024 are on the near side of the Moon; all other areas are on the far side
