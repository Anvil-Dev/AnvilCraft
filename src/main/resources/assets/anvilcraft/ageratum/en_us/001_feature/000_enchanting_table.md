# <ref item="minecraft:enchanting_table"/> Enhancement

Enchanting items made of specific materials on an enchanting table gives a higher chance of obtaining the corresponding enchantments (some of these enchantments can only be obtained by enchanting tools of the matching material on an enchanting table)

| Item                                             | Enchantments                      |
|--------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------|
| [Royal Steel Tools](../002_material/110_royal_steel.md)   | Silk Touch, Unbreaking            |
| [Frost Metal Tools](../002_material/202_frost_metal.md)   | Disintegration (exclusive)        |
| [Ember Metal Tools](../002_material/211_ember_metal.md)   | Smelting (exclusive), Fire Aspect |
| [Transcendium Tools](../002_material/312_transcendium.md) | Fortune, Looting (both can reach levels 4-5) |

[Click here](100_enchantment.md) to view new enchantments
