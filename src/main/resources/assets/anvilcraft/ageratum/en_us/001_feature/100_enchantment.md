<directory />

# Timber

- Max level: III
- Anvil cost multiplier
- Effect: When chopping, each level breaks 4 additional connected logs
- Obtained by crafting <ref item="anvilcraft:amethyst_axe"/>

# Harvest

- Max level: III
- Effect: Right-click mature crops to harvest and replant them in an area; also breaks weeds and leaves in the area; each level expands the area by one ring
- Obtained by crafting <ref item="anvilcraft:amethyst_hoe"/>

# Beheading

- Max level: III
- Effect: Each level increases the chance for mobs to drop heads; the increase is greater for skeletons and extremely high for players and the Ender Dragon
- Obtained by crafting <ref item="anvilcraft:amethyst_sword"/>

<info>
A weapon with Beheading III is guaranteed to chop off a <ref item="minecraft:dragon_head"/>
</info>

# Disintegration

- Max level: I
- Effect: When breaking a block, multiplies the dropped experience by 4, then converts each dropped item into 1 experience point
- Obtained via [special method](000_enchanting_table.md)

# Smelting

- Max level: V
- Effect: When breaking a block, smelts the drops once
- Obtained via [special method](000_enchanting_table.md)
