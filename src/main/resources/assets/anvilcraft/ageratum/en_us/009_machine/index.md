# Machine Examples

Some examples of machines that may be useful in the AnvilCraft gameplay process, to help you better understand AnvilCraft's components and design your own automated production lines. Have fun!

<tip>
The advanced machines in this handbook were carefully designed by skilled redstone builders. Don't be discouraged if you cannot come up with a better design.
</tip>
