# Spawner Crushing

This machine drops anvils onto a <ref item="minecraft:spawner"/> to spawn mobs and collect their drops. The example uses a skeleton spawner. The lower section handles the mobs and collects their drops, while the upper section repeatedly drops anvils onto the spawner.

## Advanced Spawner Crusher

The machine consists of two separate structures that can be exported or built individually. Place the lower section first, then place the upper section directly above it.

<tip>
Both sections have a 12×11-block footprint. Keep their orientation the same and align all four edges. The lower section is 10 blocks tall and the upper section is 8 blocks tall. Place the bottom layer of the upper section directly above the top layer of the lower section, with no empty layer between them. The assembled machine is 18 blocks tall.
</tip>
