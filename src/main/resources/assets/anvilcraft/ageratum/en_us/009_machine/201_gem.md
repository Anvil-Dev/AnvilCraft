## Data Decrypted: M-9201-1 "High-Precision Pipesless Gem Machine"

> From the ancient era of AnvilCraft, when fluid pipes did not yet exist — yet the ancient anvil folk still began their automation enlightenment with their own wisdom

This machine continuously swaps the work block underneath by pushing and pulling the cauldron, relying on complex and precise timing to achieve high processing speeds.

<structure id="../../structures/machine/gem_old.nbt"/>

1. Use a button to switch the copper bulb to its lit state
2. Load *equal quantities* of gem blocks and gems according to the labels

<tip>
Do not shut down the machine while it is running. Once it finishes, pull the lever down to shut it off.
</tip>
