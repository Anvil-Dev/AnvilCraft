# Hypercube

<item id="anvilcraft:hypercube"/>

## Crafting

Craft through [four-dimensional multi-block crafting](../004_block/330_spacetime_supercomputer.md#four-dimensional-multi-block-crafting):

1. Fill a 3x3x3 space with <ref item="anvilcraft:tempering_glass"/>.
2. Perform multi-block crafting once, consuming the <ref item="anvilcraft:tempering_glass"/>.
3. Repeat three times.
