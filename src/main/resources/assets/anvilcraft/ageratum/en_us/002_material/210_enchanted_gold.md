# Enchanted Gold

> Enchanted metal; craft it using [Liquid Enchantment](210_liquid_enchantment.md).

<recipe id="anvilcraft:jei/solid_liquid/enchanted_gold_ingot"/>

There are several recipes:

- 16mB blank liquid enchantment + <ref item="minecraft:gold_ingot"/> -> <ref item="anvilcraft:enchanted_gold_ingot"/>
- 4mB Mending + <ref item="minecraft:gold_ingot"/> -> <ref item="anvilcraft:enchanted_gold_ingot"/>
- 1mB Fortune + <ref item="minecraft:gold_ingot"/> -> <ref item="anvilcraft:enchanted_gold_ingot"/>

# Curse Neutralization

> Rich in luck, it can cancel the curse of *Cursed Gold* items.

<recipe id="anvilcraft:gold_ingot_from_enchanted_and_cursed"/>

- Carrying any *Enchanted Gold* item cancels all negative effects caused by *Cursed Gold* items.
- Carrying at least one stack of *Enchanted Gold* grants the Luck effect.

# Piglin Favorite

- <ref item="anvilcraft:enchanted_gold_ingot"/> can be used like a <ref item="minecraft:gold_ingot"/> for bartering with Piglins; each barter produces four sets of trade results.
- Piglins do not become hostile toward players holding *Enchanted Gold*, even if the player opens a chest in front of them (but they still retaliate when attacked).
