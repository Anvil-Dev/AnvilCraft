# Additional Effects

## <ref item="anvilcraft:weatherproof_spacesuit_helmet"/>

- Provides clear vision in lava

## <ref item="anvilcraft:weatherproof_spacesuit_chestplate"/>

- Stores an additional 160 MFE of energy, allowing flight outside a power grid
- Can be charged with a <ref item="anvilcraft:charger"/>
- When low on energy, attempts to consume a <ref item="anvilcraft:capacitor"/> from the inventory to recharge

## <ref item="anvilcraft:weatherproof_spacesuit_leggings"/>

- Provides 12 slots of [Pocket Storage](101_functional_armor.md#pocket-storage)

## <ref item="anvilcraft:weatherproof_spacesuit_boots"/>

- Can stand on **fluid sources**; hold Shift to sink
- In fluids with buoyancy that slow descent, such as bubble columns, holding Shift accelerates descent

## Set Bonus

- Grants immunity to environmental damage such as fire, lightning, freezing, and radiation
- Does not prevent void damage, but prevents falling into the void
