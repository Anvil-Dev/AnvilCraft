# <ref item="anvilcraft:breathing_helmet"/>

<recipe id="anvilcraft:breathing_helmet"/>

- When equipped as a helmet, provides the same protection as an <ref item="minecraft:iron_helmet"/>
- Lets the player breathe normally underwater or in a vacuum and removes the underwater **mining speed penalty**
- Does not prevent *suffocation by blocks*

# <ref item="anvilcraft:ionocraft_backpack"/>

<row>
<recipe id="anvilcraft:ionocraft"/>
<recipe id="anvilcraft:ionocraft_backpack"/>
</row>

- When equipped as a chestplate, provides the same protection as an <ref item="minecraft:iron_chestplate"/>
- Consumes 8 kW to operate while inside a power grid, granting the player flight
- If the player accidentally leaves the power grid while flying, they descend slowly (double-tap Space to cancel the descent)

![ionocraft_backpack.png](../../textures/ionocraft_backpack.png)

<tip>
Use the indicators beside the experience bar to tell whether you are inside a power grid
</tip>

## <ref item="anvilcraft:ionocraft"/>

- Ascends inside a power grid (consumes 2 kW); descends slowly outside a power grid
- The player can stand on it to fly

# <ref item="anvilcraft:pockets_leggings"/>

<recipe id="anvilcraft:pockets_leggings"/>

- When equipped as leggings, provides the same protection as <ref item="minecraft:iron_leggings"/>
- Provides 6 *pocket slots*

## Pocket Storage

- *Amulets* such as the <ref item="anvilcraft:topaz_amulet"/> and the <ref item="anvilcraft:amulet_box"/> work inside pocket storage
- Hold [Ctrl+F] to open the pocket wheel and select an item to swap with the offhand

# <ref item="anvilcraft:buffer_boots"/>

<recipe id="anvilcraft:buffer_boots"/>

- When equipped as boots, provides the same protection as <ref item="minecraft:iron_boots"/>
- Grants immunity to fall damage
- Hold Shift to charge for up to 1 second, then press Space to jump up to 4 times as high
