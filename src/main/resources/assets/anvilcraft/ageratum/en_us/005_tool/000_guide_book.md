# AnvilCraft Guide Book

<item id="anvilcraft:guide_book"/>


# Acquisition:

1. A player receives an <ref item="anvilcraft:guide_book"/> upon first entering a world.
2. Hold a <ref item="minecraft:anvil"/> and right-click the monolith core near the Overworld spawn, consuming the <ref item="minecraft:anvil"/> to obtain an <ref item="anvilcraft:guide_book"/>.

# Functions:

1. **Hover** your mouse over the <ref item="anvilcraft:guide_book"/> and press **A** to open AnvilCraft's "Integration", where you can view what addons AnvilCraft includes and what mods it has compat/integration with, allowing you to install them as needed
2. Use the <ref item="anvilcraft:guide_book"/> directly in-game to view most AnvilCraft information
3. Press a shortcut key on certain items to directly open the <ref item="anvilcraft:guide_book"/> and view item information
4. Adds 3 melee attack damage while held in the main hand
