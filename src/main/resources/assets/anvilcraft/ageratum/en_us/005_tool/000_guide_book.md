# AnvilCraft Guide Book

<item id="anvilcraft:guide_book"/>


# Acquisition:

- A player receives an **AnvilCraft Guide Book** upon first entering a world

- Use an anvil on the monolith core near the Overworld spawn to exchange one anvil for a guide book. The guide book cannot be crafted. Monoliths do not generate in superflat Overworlds.
- Vanilla anvils and AnvilCraft's own special anvils can be offered. The anvil appears inside the core, fades, and turns into mist rising through the line above it. Each core accepts one offering every 5 seconds.

# Functions:

1. **Hover** your mouse over the guide and press **W** to open AnvilCraft's "Integration", where you can view what addons AnvilCraft includes and what mods it has compat/integration with, allowing you to install them as needed
2. Use the guide directly in-game to view most AnvilCraft information
3. Press a shortcut key on certain items to directly open the guide and view item information
4. Adds 3 melee attack damage while held in the main hand

# Returning from the Moon:

- On the Moon, use a non-core part of the monolith, then use it again after the prompt to return to your current respawn point, following the End exit portal rules.
- Launch an upward ender pearl within 128 horizontal blocks of the lunar origin. Once it passes Y=512 at 32 blocks/tick or faster, it returns you to the ground near the Overworld origin and deals 1 damage. Returning does not require midnight.
