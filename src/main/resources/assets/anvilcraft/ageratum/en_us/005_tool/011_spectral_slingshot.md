# Usage

- Used like a <ref item="minecraft:crossbow"/> that needs no arrows, and loading does not consume ammo
- When loading, hold a weapon in the other hand. Shots deal 50% of that weapon's melee damage (only damage-boosting enchantments on the original weapon apply)

# Enchantment Notes

- Quick Charge: shortens loading time and reduces the shot cooldown
- Infinity: keeps the loaded spectral copy after firing, so no reload is needed; Shift-right-click clears the loaded copy
- Power: increases projectile damage (unlike vanilla arrows, only by 10% per level)
- Other enchantments work the same as vanilla
