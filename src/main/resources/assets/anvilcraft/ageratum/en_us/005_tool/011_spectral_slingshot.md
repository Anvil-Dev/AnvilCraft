# Usage

- Hold a melee weapon in one hand and the Spectral Slingshot in the other. Hold right-click to load a spectral copy. The original weapon stays in your hand.
- Right-click to fire the copy. Spectral projectiles fly straight and ignore gravity. Load another copy after each shot unless the Slingshot has Infinity.
- Sneak and right-click to clear the loaded copy. Keep holding right-click with another melee weapon in the opposite hand to load its copy.
- Damage: 50% of the weapon's melee damage. Only damage-boosting enchantments on the original weapon take effect. Damage can be boosted by the Slingshot's Power enchantment

# Enchantment Notes

- Quick Charge: affects loading speed, same as vanilla crossbow; also reduces cooldown by 0.25 seconds per level, up to 1 second
- Infinity keeps the spectral copy loaded after each shot, so you can fire again after the cooldown.
- Power: increases projectile damage (unlike vanilla arrows, only increases damage by 10% per level)
- Other enchantments work the same as vanilla
